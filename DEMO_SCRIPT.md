# ScamShield AI - 3 Minute Demo Script

**Objective:** A fast-paced, high-impact demonstration of ScamShield AI designed for recruiters or technical audiences.
**Tools Required:** OBS Studio or Loom, browser open to `localhost:5173`.

---

## 0:00 - 0:30 | The Pitch & Architecture
*(Screen: ScamShield Landing Page)*

**Speaker:**
"Hi, I'm [Your Name], and this is **ScamShield AI**. 
ScamShield is a production-ready cybersecurity SaaS designed to analyze and neutralize digital threats in real-time. 
Before we dive in, a quick look under the hood: The frontend is a React Single Page Application served by Nginx. The backend is powered by FastAPI and PostgreSQL, and it interfaces directly with IBM WatsonX for AI-driven threat analysis, all orchestrated via Docker."

## 0:30 - 1:00 | Authentication & Dashboard
*(Screen: Click Login -> Enter Credentials -> Dashboard)*

**Speaker:**
"Security is paramount, so the platform uses JWT-based authentication with Argon2 password hashing. 
Once logged in, we hit the Threat Center. Notice the modern, glassmorphism UI. It’s entirely responsive and built with Vanilla CSS to ensure maximum performance without the overhead of heavy UI libraries."

## 1:00 - 1:45 | Website & AI Scanner
*(Screen: Navigate to Website Scanner -> Enter `http://suspicious-login-update.com` -> Click Scan)*

**Speaker:**
"Let's look at the core feature: The AI Scanner.
If a user receives a suspicious link, they paste it here. ScamShield does two things simultaneously:
1. It queries Threat Intelligence feeds like VirusTotal.
2. It sends the payload to our IBM WatsonX LLM integration.

*(Screen: Show the Risk Score and AI Explanation)*
As you can see, the risk engine flagged this as a high-threat phishing attempt. The AI provides a human-readable breakdown of the social engineering tactics used on the page."

## 1:45 - 2:15 | QR & UPI Analyzers
*(Screen: Click on QR Scanner tab -> Enter fake QR data)*

**Speaker:**
"Attack vectors are evolving. That's why I implemented a QR Code analyzer to detect 'Quishing'—malicious payloads hidden in QR codes. I also built a UPI Analyzer, which validates Indian payment handles against known fraud databases, showcasing the platform's extensible, microservice-friendly architecture."

## 2:15 - 2:45 | Reporting & Swagger
*(Screen: Click 'Download PDF Report' -> Open PDF. Then switch tab to `localhost:8000/docs`)*

**Speaker:**
"Enterprise clients need audit trails. ScamShield automatically generates forensic PDF reports using Python's ReportLab. 
And for developers, the entire backend is self-documenting. Here is the Swagger UI, automatically generated by FastAPI, detailing our endpoints, Pydantic schemas, and security scopes."

## 2:45 - 3:00 | Conclusion
*(Screen: Switch to GitHub Repository / GitHub Actions page)*

**Speaker:**
"Finally, the entire stack is CI/CD integrated via GitHub Actions, including CodeQL security scanning and automated Docker builds. 
ScamShield isn't just an app; it's a secure, scalable platform. Thank you for watching."
